Privacy Policy – Free Pocket PDF

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app Free Pocket PDF (package com.freepocketpdf.app), published by Med Tech ("we", "us"), handles information when you use the app.

1. Your files stay on your device

All PDF and document processing happens locally on your phone. We do not upload, store, read or share your files or their contents. We do not operate any server that receives your documents.

2. No account, no personal data collected by us

The app does not require an account and we do not collect your name, e-mail address, contacts, location or any other personal information.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may automatically collect and process:

  • the device advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • device and app information (model, operating system, app version, language);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • ad interactions (impressions, clicks) and diagnostic data.

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us, and is transmitted encrypted. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required (for example in the EEA and the UK), you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization at any time in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

4. Permissions

The app only accesses the files you choose to open, through the Android file picker, and uses Internet access solely to load ads.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Data retention and deletion

We do not keep any data about you. Uninstalling the app removes all of its local data from your device. For data processed by Google AdMob, use the controls described in section 3.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: medsoft07@gmail.com

Politique de confidentialité – Free Pocket PDF

Dernière mise à jour : 4 octobre 2026

Vos fichiers restent sur votre appareil. Tout le traitement des PDF se fait localement sur votre téléphone. Nous n'envoyons, ne stockons et ne partageons aucun de vos fichiers. L'application ne demande pas de compte et nous ne collectons aucune donnée personnelle.

Publicité. L'application affiche des bannières Google AdMob. AdMob peut collecter l'identifiant publicitaire de l'appareil, des informations sur l'appareil et l'application, l'adresse IP (localisation approximative) et les interactions avec les annonces, afin de diffuser et mesurer les publicités et de prévenir la fraude. Ces données sont traitées par Google, de façon chiffrée (Règles de confidentialité de Google). Dans l'EEE, votre consentement est demandé avant toute publicité personnalisée. Vous pouvez réinitialiser votre identifiant publicitaire dans Paramètres Android → Google → Annonces.

Enfants. L'application ne s'adresse pas aux enfants de moins de 13 ans.
